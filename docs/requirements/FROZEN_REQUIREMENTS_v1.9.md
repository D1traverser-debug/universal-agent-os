# Clean-Slate v9 — Frozen Requirements Delta v1.9

Status: `FROZEN_BASELINE_V1_9 / REQUIREMENTS_AUTHORITY`
Date: 2026-10-15

v1.9 extends v1.0-v1.8 with explicit registered-child inheritance and parent-assisted product review requirements.

## V19-01 — Registered child Agents inherit current cognition semantics
Every registered child Agent that declares `UAOS_HOSTED` common-capability inheritance must consume the current UAOS adaptive-deliberation contract rather than a stale or copied local version.

For every detected problem, the child session must inherit the current problem-response floor: concrete diagnosis, macro goal/topology alignment, governing-assumption challenge and relevant external research. Familiarity is not a waiver. Risk may add counterexample search and independent verification.

## V19-02 — Registered child Agents inherit governed evolution without surrendering domain ownership
Every registered child evaluates detected problems for learning/evolution impact. Domain-owned business-rule evolution remains domain-owned; common/cross-domain evolution remains UAOS-owned.

A child may implement domain-specific learning/eval hooks, but must not create a second parent controller/evolution catalog. One observation may create a candidate but does not auto-promote to a global/domain-wide rule without scope/generalization and regression/eval evidence.

## V19-03 — Parent-assisted product requirements and architecture review
UAOS provides a reusable product-engineering capability for new domain products, major rebuilds, material feature families and failures that expose missing/incorrect product assumptions.

The review starts from the user outcome and observed/desired functions, fills missing requirements before implementation, and covers at minimum:
- functional requirements;
- non-functional constraints;
- workflow/state and authority ownership;
- failure/recovery semantics;
- evidence/assurance boundaries;
- acceptance/eval criteria;
- explicit non-goals and scope boundaries.

The resulting domain product requirements and architecture remain authoritative in the child repository. UAOS owns the common method, not the child's business specification.

## V19-04 — Inheritance must be verifiable, not declarative only
A child claiming current cognition/evolution/product-governance inheritance must expose current binding/capability metadata and regression/architecture tests that verify at least:
- direct child activation remains intact;
- required current common-capability IDs exist;
- the problem-response research/reflection floor is present;
- domain-vs-parent evolution ownership is explicit;
- product requirements/architecture authority exists at the domain scope;
- no parent/child business-identity equality or mandatory live parent business proxy is introduced.

## V19-05 — No policy copy fork
Children must reference the current UAOS common-capability/deliberation authority rather than copying the entire parent cognition/evolution policy into a competing child controller. Child documents may record domain-specific adaptation and acceptance rules only.
