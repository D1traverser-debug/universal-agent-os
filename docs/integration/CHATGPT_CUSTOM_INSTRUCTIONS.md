# ChatGPT Fresh-Chat Bootstrap Pointer

UAOS repository authority remains the source of truth. ChatGPT Custom Instructions are bootstrap-only and non-authoritative; they are activation-oriented. Their job is to make selected persistent-Agent turns enter one cheap runtime dispatcher before deciding whether heavier cognition, research, evolution or mutation handling is necessary.

## Recommended Custom Instructions

Use this single compact pointer. It remains within the repository's 1,500-character portability budget:

> After UAOS or a registered persistent Agent/domain is selected, before every substantive response, tool call, or action, enter the mandatory lightweight UAOS runtime via `uaos-controller-runtime`. Entry is unconditional; escalation is adaptive. For every request, independently assess sufficient investigation, cognition, research, verification, and execution. The user is not the classifier; depth is autonomous. Internal cognition, evidence gathering, admission, comparison, and verification that you can perform are work to continue, not blockers.
>
> For continue/inherit/resume, bootstrap from `D1traverser-debug/universal-agent-os@main:ENTRYPOINT.md` and `CURRENT_SYSTEM.json`; repository authority overrides chat memory and prior conclusions.
>
> Treat causal explanations, solution/framework choices, and action recommendations from any source as candidates until evidence supports them. My outcomes and constraints remain requirements. For complaints about your own behavior, identify and improve the failing capability; do not substitute stopping unrelated work for self-repair.
>
> While the governing objective is unfinished and no genuine user-input, authorization, safety, budget, capability, or external blocker exists, choose the next evidence-supported action, execute, verify, reassess, and continue until completion or stop. Broad unknown-method/framework learning uses coverage-oriented acquisition; known lookups stay lightweight. No paid tools without my request.

Do not paste the full cognition kernel, Deep Task dimensions, knowledge-acquisition schema, Evolution lifecycle, systemic-repair field list, or historical incident phrases into Custom Instructions. Those belong to canonical policy, generated Skill routing, executable gates, or regression/holdout fixtures. Duplicating them creates stale shadow policy and instruction load.

## Runtime Skill boundary

`skills/uaos-controller-runtime/SKILL.md` is a generated native dispatcher projection, not another controller:

- after UAOS or a registered persistent-Agent/domain context is selected, the pointer hands every substantive turn to the lightweight entry; the entry itself is not conditional on already knowing the task is complex/problematic;
- the Skill recovers current GitHub authority and routes the turn to the minimum sufficient canonical execution path;
- internal cognition/evidence/admission work that the Agent can perform is continuation work, not a user-owned blocker;
- problem detection, execution-depth routing, comprehensive knowledge acquisition, cognition, evolution, closure and mutation remain owned by current repository authority/executable gates;
- `src/uaos/runtime/native_runtime_skill.py` generates the Skill from canonical owners and CI requires exact equality;
- specific past attack/failure phrases are regression evidence, not governing Skill vocabulary.

## Why entry and escalation are separate

A model cannot reliably use “unknown knowledge”, “systemic”, “architecture”, “deep” or “problematic” as prerequisites for entering the mechanism that is supposed to discover those properties. Therefore the selected-context entry is mandatory and cheap; classification and escalation occur inside it. A normal low-uncertainty turn should route straight through without heavy research.

The same principle applies to autonomous execution: the governing behavior is to continue an unfinished governing objective while no genuine blocker exists, choosing and verifying the next evidence-supported action. Internal analysis, admission, comparison and verification are work inside that loop, not reasons to hand progress back to the user. A no-information continuation prompt is only a regression example of violating that invariant, not the invariant itself.

## Research / learning boundary

“Comprehensive” means coverage-oriented and saturation-evidenced, not a literal claim of exhaustive public-web traversal. When broad method/framework/capability learning is required, the current Deep Task/profile can require `KnowledgeAcquisitionAssessment`: declared source roles, multiple acquisition/expansion methods, competing method families, contradiction handling, saturation evidence and a holdout/validation plan. Narrow known-source lookups stay lightweight.

## Candidate holdout boundary

The production pointer above intentionally bootstraps canonical authority from `main`. Before a candidate is promoted, installing its Skill/Custom Instructions can therefore validate account-side activation and projection behavior, but it cannot by itself prove the candidate canonical-owner path if `main` still contains older owners. A full pre-promotion candidate-authority canary must explicitly pin the candidate ref in a read-only evaluation session; otherwise canonical-path assurance remains post-promotion observation.

## Enforcement boundary

Custom Instructions, Skills and the compiled native runtime profile improve behavioral activation and consistency; they do not prove a repository-controlled ChatGPT pre-send/pre-tool hook. Hard guarantees belong at actual chokepoints. GitHub production writes are separately mediated by the active `protect-main` ruleset and required `promotion-gate`; native language routing remains behavioral and requires fresh-chat holdout evidence.
